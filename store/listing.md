## Full description
Poker Face Lab maps a face in real time and shows how much it is reacting, moment by moment.

Point the camera at yourself, or upload a short clip, and watch:

• A live 3D wireframe of 478 points on the face, glowing where it moves
• A reaction gauge that compares the face with its own resting baseline
• Which zones are moving: brows, eyes, nose, mouth, jaw and head
• The expression on show, from happy to surprised
• Blinks a minute, gaze darts and peak reaction over time
• "Moments spotted", each with a science check on the old body-language tales

It's for fun and curiosity. Poker Face Lab measures how much a face moves. It cannot tell you whether someone is lying, and it says so: decades of studies have found no facial movement that reliably signals a lie. Treat the gauge as a conversation starter, never as proof.

Private by design: the face model runs entirely on your phone. Your video is never uploaded, saved or shared, and the app has no accounts, ads or tracking.

Please only analyse your own face or the faces of people who have agreed to it.
