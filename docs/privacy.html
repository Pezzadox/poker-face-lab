Poker Face Lab privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026. Published by The Pezzadox Show.

The short version: Poker Face Lab collects no personal data. Video from your camera or from a clip you choose is analysed on your phone, in memory, and is never uploaded, saved or shared.

What the app does with video

When you use the camera or choose a clip, the app reads the video frame by frame on your device. A face model that ships inside the app maps points on the face and works out how much each part of the face moves. The video frames, the face points and the scores are kept in your phone's memory only while the app is open. Nothing is written to storage and nothing leaves your device. When you close the app or start a new clip, it is gone.

Camera permission

The app asks for the camera only when you tap Use camera or Record with camera. You can say no and still upload a clip from your gallery. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or contact details.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising ID.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone, because there is no data to share.
  • No face recognition: the app does not try to identify who a person is.
  • No lie detection: the app says clearly that facial movement cannot show whether someone is lying.

Use it fairly

Please only analyse your own face or the faces of people who have agreed to it.

Children

Poker Face Lab is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
