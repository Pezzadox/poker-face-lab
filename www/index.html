Example

    Nothing yet. Moments appear here once the baseline is set.

    How it works. A face model maps 478 points on the face. The first few seconds set a resting baseline. After that the lab watches for movements that stand out from that baseline: brows, eyelids, gaze, nostrils, lips and head.

    What it can't do. Decades of studies have found no facial movement that reliably signals a lie. Treat the gauge as a conversation starter, never as proof.
